# LoopDay

**A tiny habit tracker that forgives you**

> #11 of 33 in the build order · Easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Habits, Health & Learning

## What it is

Track 3–10 habits with one tap a day. Streaks you can recover, a clean weekly view and simple stats. No social feed, no coaching, no clutter.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Daily use is the best retention there is, and the market is huge. The angle is simplicity and forgiving streaks, for people who gave up on complicated habit apps. It also proves out the shared Progression Loop module that every other app uses.

## Who it's for

People who have tried and quit complicated habit apps.

**The problem:** Most habit apps are overwhelming, and one missed day wipes out a streak along with the motivation.

## Version one

- 3 free habits (up to 10 with premium)
- One-tap check-off and a weekly view
- Streaks with 'recovery mode' and earned freezes
- Reminders
- Simple completion stats

**Example:** A user tracks water, walking, reading and no-scroll evenings, misses Tuesday, and uses a freeze to keep their streak.

## Later
